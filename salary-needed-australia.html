How the salary is calculated

Formula

Loan = median house price × (1 − deposit). Monthly repayment uses a standard principal-and-interest formula at the chosen rate and term. Salary needed = annual repayments ÷ the chosen share of gross income. Defaults: 20% deposit, 30-year loan, 6.74% p.a., repayments capped at 30% of gross household income.

What is left out

  • Stamp duty, legal fees and lenders mortgage insurance. In NSW, duty on a $1.66M house is about $70K on top of the deposit.
  • Council rates, strata and insurance. The US version of this chart includes them; Australian affordability measures usually count repayments only.
  • Banks also test you at your rate + 3 percentage points (APRA's serviceability buffer), so borrowing limits are tighter than this chart suggests.

Read the numbers with care

  • Capital cities use one source (Domain, Sep qtr 2026). Suburbs and regional towns come from several trackers with different windows and methods. Each row in the table names its source.
  • Suburb medians in prestige areas rest on few sales (Point Piper: 7), so they jump around.
  • Rows marked ≈ are older or approximate figures.
